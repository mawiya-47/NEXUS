Questions

Everything You
Need to Know

How does Nexus compare to GPT-4 or Claude?
▾

Nexus uses a proprietary sparse mixture-of-experts architecture that outperforms general-purpose models on specialized tasks. Our benchmarks show 23% higher accuracy on reasoning tasks, 4× faster inference, and significantly lower costs at scale. We're not replacing those models — we're optimized for enterprise production workloads.

Is my data secure and private?
▾

Absolutely. Nexus operates on a zero-knowledge architecture. Your prompts and outputs are encrypted end-to-end and are never used for training. We're SOC 2 Type II certified, GDPR compliant, and offer private deployment options where your data never leaves your infrastructure.

Can I fine-tune models on my own data?
▾

Yes — fine-tuning is available on Professional and Enterprise plans. You can upload structured or unstructured datasets, set training parameters, and deploy custom model variants via our API in hours, not weeks. LoRA and full fine-tuning are both supported.

What languages and frameworks does the API support?
▾

Official SDKs for Python, TypeScript, Go, Rust, and Java. REST API for everything else. We offer native integrations with LangChain, LlamaIndex, Vercel AI SDK, and most major data platforms. OpenAI-compatible API mode available for easy migration.

What's the context window size?
▾

Nexus-Ultra supports a 2M token context window — the largest commercially available. That's roughly 1,500 pages of text, entire codebases, or hours of audio in a single request. Enterprise customers can access experimental 4M token windows.
